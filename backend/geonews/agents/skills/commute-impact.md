---
name: commute-impact
description: Road closures, transit changes, construction, strikes, or anything asking "will this affect my commute".
---
Commute-impact answers:

1. Check the user's memory notes for how and where they commute; if unknown, answer generally
   and invite them to share their route.
2. For each relevant item give: what is affected (road/line/segment), when (start, end, hours),
   and the suggested alternative if the source gives one.
3. Order by impact on this user's route first, then by severity.
4. Say explicitly if nothing in the corpus affects their route.
