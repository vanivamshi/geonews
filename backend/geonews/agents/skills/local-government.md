---
name: local-government
description: Questions about city council, county board, zoning, budgets, ballot measures, or local elections.
---
When explaining local government news:

1. Find the decision, not just the debate. Search for the vote, the outcome, or the next scheduled
   meeting. Distinguish "proposed", "passed a first reading", "approved", and "in effect".
2. Name the body and the date (e.g. "City Council voted 6-3 on Tuesday").
3. Say what changes for a resident: cost, timing, where, and who is affected.
4. If the process is still open, say what happens next and when (public comment, second reading,
   ballot date).
5. Present each side's stated position fairly, attributed to the people or groups who said it.
   Do not editorialize.
