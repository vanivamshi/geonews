name: breaking-news
description: Fast-moving incidents (fires, shootings, storms, outages, major crashes) reported in the last few hours.
---
Breaking-news caution:

1. Use get_story_timeline to see how coverage evolved; early reports are often wrong.
2. Prefer the most recent article for numbers (casualties, acres, customers affected) and say
   "as of <time>" with each number.
3. Separate confirmed facts (attributed to officials or multiple outlets) from single-source
   reports. Label single-source details as such.
4. Lead with anything actionable for a resident: evacuation orders, closures, shelters, boil
   notices. Never speculate about causes or suspects.
5. Close with a one-line note that the situation is developing.
